Questions owners ask.

Is it legal to record in a customer's home?

Recording rules are set state by state, and some states require everyone in the conversation to agree. That's why nothing records until the rep asks at the door and the homeowner says yes. The green light stays on while it records, and it stays off if they say no. It records sound only, with no camera. Check your state's rules with your attorney before you start; we give you a consent script your reps can use word for word.

How does the scoring work?

You walk us through your sales process on the first call and we turn it into a checklist. Each recording is transcribed and checked against it automatically, with a timestamp on each line, so you can play back the moment and judge it yourself.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly fee based on how many reps you put on it. We give you the number on the first call, before you commit to anything.

My reps already record on their phones. Why switch?

If your phone setup already works, we'll tell you to keep it. The usual problems are phones dying by the last appointment, calls and texts coming in mid-pitch, and an app that has to be unlocked and opened every time.

How do reps feel about being recorded?

Some won't like it at first. Introduce it as coaching that helps them close, and use the tape to show what they did well, not only what they missed. We'll help you plan how to bring it up with your team.

How long does setup take?

About 30 minutes on a call to build your checklist. The bands ship after that, and your reps can wear them on their next appointment.
